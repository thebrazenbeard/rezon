# Rezon brand rationale

The mark shows three open facets aimed at one central proposition. The facets represent heterogeneous reasoning methods that inspect the same subject without collapsing into one voice; the open geometry represents falsifiability rather than enclosure or certainty.

The light and dark logos share identical geometry. The composer icon keeps the same three-facet structure with fewer visual dependencies so it remains legible at small sizes. All assets are self-contained SVG with no external fonts, scripts, linked resources, or remote references.
